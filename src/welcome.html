Private by default, with ads and trackers already blocked and idle tabs freeing their memory. Here's what's new, and two quick things to set up if you want them.

Get set up

Bring your bookmarksOnly bookmarks are copied, never passwords
Open links in RiffleWindows asks you to confirm

New in this version

Arrange your tabs
Drag to reorder, or drag a tab away from the strip to give it its own window. Right-click a tab for more.
Your toolbar, your way
Choose which icons show beside the address bar in Settings, Toolbar.
Pinned tabs
They stay small, stay awake and come back next time.
Search tabs
Press Ctrl Shift A to find any open tab, even a sleeping one.
Reader mode
A book icon appears in the address bar on articles and shows just the text.
System volume
The music button now controls Windows' volume, as well as each tab's.
Float a video
Press Ctrl Shift P to pop a playing video out in its own small window.
Zoom that remembers
Each site keeps its own level. Press Ctrl and plus or minus.
Blocked count
The shield shows what it stopped on this page and this month, counted only on your PC.
Extensions
Add an extension from an unpacked folder, using the puzzle icon.
Clear errors
Plain words for no internet, slow connections and sites that are down, with one click to try again.
